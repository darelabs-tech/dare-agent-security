# Code of Conduct

DARE Agent Security is committed to a professional, technically rigorous, and respectful collaboration environment.

## Expected behavior

Participants should:

- discuss ideas and evidence rather than attack people;
- assume good faith while remaining rigorous about security claims;
- disclose conflicts of interest when relevant;
- avoid publishing third-party confidential information;
- respect coordinated vulnerability disclosure processes;
- keep discussions focused on the project and its technical goals;
- welcome reproducible disagreement and independent validation.

## Unacceptable behavior

The following are not acceptable in project spaces:

- harassment, threats, or discriminatory abuse;
- doxxing or publication of private information;
- deliberate disclosure of secrets, customer data, or embargoed vulnerabilities;
- encouragement of unauthorized attacks against third-party systems;
- knowingly fabricated security findings or manipulated evidence;
- repeated disruption of technical discussion.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, hide, or remove content and may restrict participation when necessary to protect contributors, users, customers, or responsible security processes.

Security reports must follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) rather than public issue discussions.
